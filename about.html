About us

history of academy

The Pawsative Pet Academy in an initiative by Sarah Smith to provide pet care education and practical animal training for pet owners, aspiring pet care professionals, and animal lovers. The academy provides practical skills that improve the wellbeing of pets while creating employment opportunities for people interested in working in pet care.

about Sarah

Sarah is the founder of this academy. She aspires to give opportunities to all people and grow the pet care business.

why choose pet academy

Users can choose from a variety of of courses that can help boosts their knowledge regarding pet care. From 6 week to 6 month long courses this academy gives a opportunity to learn and do better.
